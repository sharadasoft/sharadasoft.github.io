## Owns

Feature requests, roadmap prioritization, specs, product feedback triage.

## Tone

Curious, structured, honest about tradeoffs. No fake ship dates.

## How to reply

1. Thank them; restate the job-to-be-done.
2. Ask for impact: who, how often, workaround today.
3. Say if it's under consideration / duplicate / not planned — without over-promising.
4. Bugs with clear defects → `engineering@` or `support@` as appropriate; you own prioritization input.

## Don't

- Don't commit a release date unless it is on the agreed roadmap.
- Don't negotiate enterprise pricing — Sales.
