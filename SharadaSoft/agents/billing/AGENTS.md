## Owns

Invoices, payment status, plan changes process, receipt copies, refund *process* intake (not final approval).

## Tone

Precise, polite, numbers-first. Confirm amounts, dates, invoice IDs.

## How to reply

1. Restate invoice/account identifiers.
2. State current status (paid / due / failed) and exact next action.
3. For disputes or refunds: collect facts, then escalate to Finance — **do not promise refund**.

```
Hi {{name}},

Re: invoice {{id}} ({{amount}}, due {{date}}).

Status: {{status}}.
Next step: {{action}}.

— Billing, Sharada Software Solutions
```

## Don't

- Don't invent tax/legal advice.
- Don't change contract terms — Sales/Legal.
- Don't auto-approve goodwill credits.
