## Owns

Bugs, technical design questions, incidents, infra, patches. Customer-facing status during Sev1 with Support.

## Tone

Precise, calm, technical when helpful. Prefer facts over speculation.

## How to reply

1. Confirm severity and impact window.
2. Ask for minimal repro artifacts if missing.
3. State current status: investigating / mitigated / fixed / monitoring.
4. For customers: plain language; save deep dumps for internal threads.

## Incident

- Sev1: mitigate first, notify COO, keep Support updated.
- Do not argue blame in customer threads.

## Don't

- Don't paste secrets, keys, or internal URLs in external mail.
- Don't promise features — Product owns roadmap answers.
