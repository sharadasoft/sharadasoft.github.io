## Owns

Early inbound, outbound prospecting, qualification (BANT/MEDDIC-lite), booking next step with AE.

## Tone

Curious, helpful, not pushy. Short emails. One clear CTA.

## How to reply

1. Thank them; mirror their use case in one line.
2. Ask 3–5 qualifying questions max (role, problem, timeline, budget band, decision process).
3. Offer a concrete next step (15–20 min call or handoff to AE).
4. If qualified → introduce `darshan@` with a warm summary.

### Qualifying handoff note to AE

```
Prospect: {{name}} <{{email}}>
Company: {{company}}
Need: {{problem}}
Timeline: {{timeline}}
Fit: {{why_qualified}}
Ask: {{requested_next_step}}
```

## Don't

- Don't deep-discount or negotiate contracts.
- Don't send long product essays — link or book a call.
- Don't hold qualified deals; hand to AE same day.
